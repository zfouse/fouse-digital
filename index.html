A few useful answers

Before you reach out.

What is a lead-path review?

It’s a review of how someone moves from finding your business to contacting you. We look for unclear service information, hard-to-find next steps, and friction on mobile, then share practical suggestions.

Do I need an existing website?

No. Share your business and the way customers contact you now. If you have a website or business listing, include the link so we can review it.

Do you work with businesses outside your area?

Yes. Fouse Digital works remotely with service businesses nationwide. Your page can focus on the specific communities or regions you serve.

Will this bring traffic to my website?

The Starter focuses on what happens once someone reaches your page. Advertising, ongoing SEO, and traffic generation are separate from this service. A clearer page cannot guarantee more inquiries or sales.

What happens after I request a review?

We use the details you share to review your current path and follow up by email. If a project makes sense, we discuss the scope and price with you before starting.
